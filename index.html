How to use this site

  • Read HubSpot first — it carries the strongest signal because the connector lifecycle pattern (§0 of its README) maps directly to SpecterX's Gmail and Outlook connectors.
  • Use the platform pages to jump straight to any saved page. Each row has a "View offline" link that opens the saved HTML with all original images intact.
  • Open Compare for side-by-side voice samples and structural patterns across all four platforms.
  • The per-platform README.md is the long-form analysis. The platform pages excerpt the key takeaways.

Phase status

Phase 1 (this site) is the prototype output. Phase 2 (post-checkpoint) will expand the crawl to ~80–150 pages per platform and produce the synthesis guidelines (STYLE_GUIDE.md, PAGE_TEMPLATE.md, SCREENSHOT_GUIDELINES.md, INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md).
