Recipient section — article split decisions

The most directly comparable section to SpecterX's §3 "Receive files". Virtru's decisions validate or challenge our own splits.

Virtru pattern
"Read a Virtru Encrypted Email" — main article covering all verification methods inline (choose SSO or Send Verification Email).

"Using SMS for secondary verification" — separate article because the SMS flow is meaningfully different (phone number must be registered, OTP arrives by text).

"Reply to a Virtru Encrypted Email" — separate article because the reply action is a distinct workflow from reading.

"Share a Virtru Secured File without Virtru Installed" — separate article for the case where the recipient wants to re-share.
Implication for SpecterX §3
Main article: "Open a SpecterX-protected file" covers common case (email verification).

Supplementary: "Verify with SMS" and "Verify with a personal secret" as separate articles (linked from main article).

This modifies our earlier decision to fully merge all verification into one article — Virtru says split when the flow is meaningfully different.

"Send a file back to the sender" stays separate ✅ (Virtru validates Reply as separate).

6 unique patterns not found in the other 4 reference KBs

  • Recipient as a top-level audience section. Not buried inside sender docs. Recipients are a separate audience who may not know the product at all.
  • Connector disambiguation FAQ article. "What is the difference between Outlook 365 Add-in and Outlook Desktop Extension?" answers the install-decision question without a cross-connector hub. Scales cleanly to SpecterX's Outlook Classic vs Outlook New split.
  • H4 "Please Note:" cross-connector callout. Lightweight disambiguation at the top of connector articles. Less prominent than a callout box; more than a parenthetical.
  • Advanced Products isolated section with a standard article set. Each enterprise deployment product (gateway, keystore, etc.) gets: About → Prerequisites → Install → Post-install → Reference. Page structure is entirely predictable.
  • Compliance & Legal articles per regulatory framework. HIPAA, CJIS, FERPA, FIPS, ITAR each get a dedicated article. Enterprise security customers search by framework name.
  • Per-product release notes in a dedicated section, separate from the product's own documentation section. Keeps the main nav clean.

Key takeaways for SpecterX

  • §3 "Receive files" as top-level section ✅ — Virtru validates. Recipients are a first-class audience.

  • Add compliance articles (HIPAA, GDPR) and per-product release notes to §11 Reference.
