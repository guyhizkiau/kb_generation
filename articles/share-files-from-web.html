Securely share a file from the SpecterX Web Platform

The SpecterX Web Platform lets you upload files, assign security policies, and share protected links with recipients — all from your browser. This article walks you through the complete sharing workflow, from uploading your file to sending the protected link and understanding what your recipient sees.

Before you get started

  • Requirements. A SpecterX account with a Collaborator or Administrator role. Contact your system administrator if you don't have access.
  • Permissions required. You need Collaborator or higher permissions to upload and share files. Viewer-only users cannot initiate shares.
  • Supported file types. Most office documents (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx), PDFs, and image files are supported. Contact your administrator for the full list of supported formats in your organization's configuration.
  • Limitations. Individual files up to 30 GB can be uploaded. Sharing with recipients outside your organization requires that your administrator has not restricted external sharing in the active policy.

Upload a file and open the Share dialog

When you log in to the SpecterX Web Platform, you arrive at the My Files view. This is where your protected files live.

1

In the left navigation, click Share files (the teal button at the top of the sidebar).

A dropdown appears with two options: Share a file and Share a folder.

2

Click Share a file. The Share dialog opens.

The dialog has two steps shown at the top: 1 — Add items and Recipients and 2 — Select Policy. You are now on Step 1.

3

In the file drop zone on the left, either drag your file into the area, or click Choose a file or drag it here and browse to select a file from your computer.

Files up to 30 GB are supported. A progress bar appears while the file uploads. Wait until the bar turns green before proceeding.

Add recipients and set permissions

1

In the Share with field on the right side of the dialog, type a recipient's email address and press Enter.

Repeat for each recipient. Each address appears as a chip below the field.

2

Under the email field, click the Contributor dropdown to set the permission level for recipients. Options are:

  • Viewer — can view files but cannot upload or modify them.
  • Contributor — can view, upload, and download files (subject to policy). This is the default.
  • Co-Owner — full access, including managing permissions and policies.
Note: The permission level applies to all recipients you added in the current Share dialog. To set different permission levels for different recipients, share in multiple steps.

Select a security policy

1

Once you have added at least one recipient and your file has finished uploading, click Next in the bottom-right corner of the dialog.

You advance to Step 2: Select Policy.

2

From the policy dropdown, choose the security policy that matches the sensitivity of the data you are sharing. The policies available in this list are defined by your administrator.

Common policies include:

  • Default — standard access with tracking. Suitable for most internal sharing.
  • HIPAA Compliance / highly sensitive — includes watermarking, phone verification, and disabled forwarding.
  • Email verification — requires the recipient to verify their email address with a one-time code before accessing the file.
Note: If your administrator has assigned a policy for your organization, it may be pre-selected. Always confirm the policy is appropriate before sharing.
3

If the selected policy requires a phone number (for SMS verification), a phone number field appears for each recipient. Enter the recipient's mobile number in international format (e.g. +1 212 555 0100).

Complete the share and copy the link

1

Click Share Files in the bottom-right corner to complete the share. SpecterX sends an email notification to each recipient with a protected link.

The dialog closes and you return to the My Files view. Your newly shared file appears in the file list.

2

To copy the protected link without re-opening the share dialog, click the share icon (the arrow icon) next to the file name in the file list.

A small dialog appears showing the protected link URL. Click Copy to copy it to your clipboard. You can then paste this link into an email, Teams message, or any other channel.

Security note: The link shown in the Copy dialog is a tracked, policy-enforced link. Anyone who receives this link is subject to the verification and access controls defined by the security policy you selected. If you need to revoke access, see Update permissions after sharing below.

Recipient experience

When your recipient receives the notification email from SpecterX, they click the protected link and arrive at the SpecterX Recipient Page. What they see depends on the policy you applied.

  • Email verification policy. A prompt asks them to enter their email address to receive a one-time access code. After entering the code, the file opens in the SpecterX Viewer.
  • Phone (SMS) verification policy. A prompt asks them to enter the last four digits of their phone number. An SMS code is sent; they enter it to access the file.
  • Password-protected policy. The recipient must enter a password. The sender defines this password during the share flow.
  • Default (no verification). The recipient clicks the link and the file opens directly in the SpecterX Viewer.

From the SpecterX Viewer, recipients can view the file online, and — if the policy permits — download a copy, open it in a supported external application, or forward the link.

Note: Forwarded links remain subject to the same policy as the original share. If the policy disables further sharing, the recipient will not see a forward or share button in the viewer.

Update permissions after sharing

1

In My Files, find the file whose permissions you want to change. Click the share icon (arrow) next to the file name.

The Share with dialog opens, showing the protected link and a Who has access link.

2

Click Who has access to open the Share & Permissions Drawer. This panel lists every recipient and their current permission level.

3

To change a recipient's permission, click the role dropdown next to their name and select the new level.

To remove a recipient entirely, click the delete icon next to their name. The protected link remains active for other recipients, but the removed user can no longer access the file.

4

To change the policy applied to the file, use the Parent policy dropdown in the Share & Permissions Drawer. The new policy takes effect immediately.
