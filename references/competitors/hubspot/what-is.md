# Coverage notes (paraphrased)

HubSpot's equivalent of a "What is X?" article is a hub page titled "Get Started" rather than a definition article. It treats the platform-overview problem as a navigation problem: instead of one long page, it routes the reader to a glossary plus per-job-to-be-done sections.

Sections in order: HubSpot Glossary, Set up your account, Manage your CRM database, Market your business to generate leads, Generate sales, Support your customers, Build your website, Report on performance, Automate your processes, Join the Community, Learn with HubSpot Academy.

What HubSpot is: described in one line as a platform connecting marketing, sales, and service tools to a unified CRM database. The product definition is short; the value comes from showing the breadth of jobs the platform covers.

Concepts introduced: CRM database, contacts/companies/deals, marketing campaigns, sales engagement, support tickets, CMS / website builder, dashboards, workflows/automation, Academy certifications, the Community. A separate Glossary page is linked as the canonical place to learn vocabulary — a useful pattern for SpecterX to consider.

The page does NOT say what HubSpot is not, and does not position against competitors.

Reader framing: a business owner or admin standing up an account for the first time; addressed as "you" with possessive "your account" / "your team." Tone is instructional, accessible, calm. No screenshots — uses nine branded icons as visual section anchors. Estimated 200-250 words on the hub page itself; the linked sub-pages do the heavy lifting.
